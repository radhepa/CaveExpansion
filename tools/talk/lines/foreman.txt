## foreman.past
I started as a cart-hauler. Pushed Grumble's grandmother up and down a drift for three shifts.
Tamsin taught me to sound a roof. Tap, listen, trust nothing. Tamsin went down with the Buried Seam.
The Buried Seam was rich. Too rich. They cut the ore out of the pillars, and the roof came down on all of them.
I was meant to be on that shift. Took a fever the week before. I've never forgiven that fever. Or thanked it enough.
Young Pell was on the Seam too. First shift. Pell's first lantern was never found. I think of it at the empty hook.
Eleven shifts as foreman. Never lost a delver. I mean to keep it so.
I shored up a Deepwatch post once. Stiff folk. Decent. Don't wait for them to laugh; you'll be waiting a while.
Hesk made me foreman. Said I worried enough for two. Turns out you need to worry enough for four.
Before this camp I worked a copper hole so wet we bailed more than we dug.
After the Seam I went to the Guildhall and stood on the long table till they wrote the pillar rule.
The hermit below was Guild once. A better delver than me. Stayed down one shift and never came up. Can't say I blame the choice.
